THE MISSION

Three sectors. Three commanders. One galaxy.

Fight through the Outer Rim, Crimson Nebula, and Void Citadel. Every fifth kill drops repair, energy, or shield salvage. Survive 30 seconds in each sector to challenge its commander. Boss victories repair your hull and permanently upgrade your weapons for this run. Dash grants brief invulnerability; nova clears hostile shots and blasts every enemy. The Aegis fighter adds twin wing cannons, a salvage magnet, aim assist, and a six-second overdrive that doubles firing speed and boosts damage. Switch aim assist or camera auto-fire off below the arena for manual combat. Complete all three sectors to win.
