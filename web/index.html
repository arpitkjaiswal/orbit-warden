THE MISSION

Three sectors. Three commanders. One galaxy.

Fight through the Outer Rim, Crimson Nebula, and Void Citadel. Every fifth kill drops repair, energy, or shield salvage. Survive 30 seconds in each sector to challenge its commander. Boss victories repair your hull and permanently upgrade your weapons for this run. Dash grants brief invulnerability; nova clears hostile shots and blasts every enemy. Choose Vanguard, Bastion, or Spectre. Nine combat waves, armed interceptors, and elite carriers stand between you and three commanders with evolving attacks. Fire guided missile salvos with C, pick one of three upgrades at each refit station, and build your own winning combination of weapons, hull, drones, and energy systems. Six pilot badges track your achievements on this device. Switch aim assist or camera auto-fire off below the arena for manual combat. Complete all three sectors to win.

Gesture guide and camera help

Start a mission or open Control Lab, then enable the camera (G). Keep one whole hand visible in good light. Tap Center my hand in a comfortable position. Relaxed steering controls left/right; choose Free flight to use both axes. Higher Hand reach means smaller gestures move farther. Pinch thumb/index with other fingers open for 0.45s, hold a peace sign for 0.8s, or extend index/middle/ring with pinky folded for 0.55s to launch missiles; release before repeating. Aim assist and camera auto-fire help you fight. Palm and fist stop automatic fire. If tracking disappears, hand movement and powers stop; the mission pauses after 0.8 seconds unless you turn that option off. Use keyboard or touch at any time. HTTPS or localhost is required; allow camera permission. Camera processing happens on your device.
